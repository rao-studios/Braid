The model

Braid
A language model made of strands under one reed. Every token's probability is split exactly among the strands, and each Thread's part is cited to its own text.
Strand
One member of a braid: the commons or a Thread. Each runs as a node.
The commons
The pretrained Mamba2 of 130 million parameters, run as imported. The yardstick every Thread is measured against, and credited to nobody. It has no index, and its λ is 0. Its blocks 12–23, final norm and embedding are the tail every Thread runs.
Thread
One corpus, a head of blocks 0–11 trained on it, a provenance index of every position, and the commons' tail behind it. Not AII's Thread service.
Pack
The imported commons on disk, written once and re-hashed on every load. The commons is built from it whole, and its seam names the tail every Thread is trained against.
Head
A node's own blocks 0–11: trained for a Thread, the pack's own for the commons. The only weights a Thread keeps.
Tail
Blocks 12–23, the final norm and the embedding, in and out: the commons', frozen, run by every Thread behind its own head, named by the seam hash.
The cut
Where the head ends and the tail begins: 12.
The reed
The braid's one output layer: a strand's normed state times the embedding's transpose, with the padding rows masked. The same matrix reads tokens in and scores them out, as the commons was trained.
Node
A strand as a running process. At every position it sends a normed state of 768 floats and its 16 hits, and nothing else.
StateStack
A strand's whole history: every layer's SSM state and convolution window, 19,390,464 bytes at 130m, at any length.

The index and the receipt

Provenance index
Per Thread, one entry per position of its corpus: a key, the next token, both addresses, and the transition's loss. Written after training.
Key
[√½ · mid/‖mid‖, √½ · final/‖final‖], 1,536 floats. mid is the residual stream after block 12, the scheme residual:12.
Hit
One retrieved entry, logged as [entry, score, value, row, offset, loss]. The row and offset say where the value sits.
Address
(row, offset): a partition's row in a Thread's table, and a token's offset in it.
Share
A strand's part of a token's probability, w′ · p_t(y) / p(y). A token's shares sum to 1.
Citation
The partition and offset of a hit whose value is the token that was written.
Span
Three or more tokens in a row, each with a matching hit at rank three or better, walking one partition in order. Re-read from its source by verify.
Entropy
How spread out a belief is, −Σ p ln p, in nats. An even spread over N outcomes gives ln N: sixteen even words give 2.77, and every doubling adds ln 2, 0.69. Divided by ln 2 it is in bits, so sixteen even words are 4 bits. e to the entropy, the perplexity, reads it back as the number of outcomes in play.
Nat, bit
Units of entropy. A nat counts with the natural logarithm, the unit Braid logs; a bit counts in base two, one yes-or-no question. One nat is about 1.44 bits.
H_lm
The entropy of a strand's reed over the vocabulary, in nats: the claim.
H_knn
The entropy of a strand's 16 hits, weighted by softmax(score/τ) and grouped by value: the evidence. At most ln 16.
Lift
ln p_t(y) − ln p_c(y): how much better than the commons a strand predicted a token.
Gate, memory
The weight the braid gives each strand. A memory moved by lift at prompt tokens only, never by more than ten to one on one token.
Open floor
0.05 · 2 / max(2, N). A strand whose memory sits below it is left out of the mixture; the commons and the leader never are.
Trace
clamp((L − 24) / 72, 0, 1), where L is the longest live chain following one document in order. Logged and replayed; never applied.
Trajectory module, e_t
Reads the slope of H_knn and logs e_t. It enters the gate only through κ, [gate] module_gain, which is 0.
The β4 shadow
The trace gate m · exp(4 · (trace − mean trace)), logged beside the live gate, never applied.
Credit, royalties
Views derived from a log: form words to the commons, content words by share, weighted by bits = −log₂ p_commons(y).
Session log
JSONL, schema 1: a header, then per answer a gen line, one tok line per position, and an end line with the body's hash. The artifact braid verify replays.

AII

AII
The Ambient Intelligence Index: one per Mac, in ~/.rao, shared by every app.
The Thread service
AII's index server, on HTTP 47081 and gRPC 47090. Its code calls it Thread; this site never does.
Sewn
AII's grounded chat server. Seam is the wire between the two.
Lane
An app's share of the index, opened by the app's secret. The secret, not the request, names the lane.
Owner
A person within a lane: local-<app> when there is no account, aii-local for the Rao apps.
Group
An owner's collection, <app>-<group> on the wire.
Record
One filed value, in an aii-record/1 envelope, with the id <app>/<kind>/<recordID>.
Envelope
A record's canonical JSON: app, kind, id, title, group, tags, the app's own payload. At most 1 MB.
Partition
A piece of a record's text, about 1,500 characters, embedded on its own. The unit a Thread addresses by row.
ExportCorpus
AII's paged export of every record an owner filed, whole and in id order. How a Thread reads a lane.
Corpus hash
SHA-256 of the sorted lines of document id, partition index and text hash. The receipt for what a Thread learned from.
