Where does provenance come from?

On the index a Thread writes after training, and the two numbers it reads at every word.

The playground, step 4, the index · the proof

Provenance is not made during training. It is made right after, in one more pass over the corpus.

The trained Thread reads every record again, whole, from a blank state. At every position it stops and writes one entry into its index: a key, the token that came next, and where that token sits.

The key is a fingerprint of where in its text the Thread is. Ten ways to take it were tried on 2026-10-15. This one, the stream after the Thread's own twelve blocks and before the commons' tail, found the fact's partition most often, level with one other, and the rule fixed in advance kept the default.

608 entries for 18 records

An entry needs a word before it and a word after it. A record's last word is followed only by the end marker, so it is never a key, and its first word follows only a marker, so it is never cited. The harbour's 18 records of 626 tokens make 608 entries.

The two numbers, measured

At inference the index is asked one question at every word: which sixteen places look most like this one? The answer, with the reed's, gives two distributions.

The reed's is the claim: a probability for every token in the vocabulary, from the Thread's own state. The index's is the evidence: each of the sixteen places votes for the token that came next there, weighted by how close it is. H_lm and H_knn are their entropies.

At that first word the top place is Harlow Light's own “ In”, at a cosine of 0.99999905. The commons, which never read the record, spreads its belief across the vocabulary.

No entropy decides a citation. A token is cited when one of the sixteen places holds the very token that was written. Three or more such tokens in a row, walking one partition in order, make a span. Low H_knn makes the location sharp. It is not a gate.

For model engineers: the key, the search, the files

The key is [√½ · mid/‖mid‖, √½ · final/‖final‖], 1,536 floats, unit length, computed in f32 and stored as f16, renormalised on load. mid is the residual stream after block 12, the last output of the Thread's head before the commons' tail; final is the normed state at the top. The raw SSM state as a key half would be 789,504 bytes a position, which is why the state keys were pooled.

Search is one matrix multiply against every key, an argPartition to k + 8, and a sort to the top 16. Weights are softmax(score/τ) with τ 0.05, so H_knn never exceeds ln 16. No score is ever compared across two Threads' key spaces.

A version's index is index.safetensors with its index.json, partitions.json and checkpoints.json, each bound to the version by SHA-256. Under the sweep's probe an entry's nearest other entry lands in its own document 20.0% of the time, against 25.2% for RaoLM's transformer key and 2.5% by chance; the claim that the Mamba key separates documents as well is not made.

The index is written once, after training: every position, its next token, and its address. A citation is a lookup into it.
